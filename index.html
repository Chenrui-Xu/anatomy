ANATOMY · EXPERIMENTAL CONTRIBUTION

Words that follow the mechanism.

Choose a gauge block. The drawing, reading, and explanation change together, including the motion between stops. Hover a part or focus its name to connect the words to the mechanism.

Silent demonstration: start at 18 px, select the 10 px block, highlight the plunger, then select 14 px and highlight the needle. Values are drawing units, not calibrated physical measurements.

The prototype adds an optional explanation helper and a variant of Anatomy’s existing dial indicator. The helper has no animation loop or model of its own. It uses the same displayed state as the drawing. Text stays visible without scripts, and reduced motion jumps directly to the selected reading.
